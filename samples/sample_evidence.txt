Municipal Water Complaint File - Ward 14

Complaint log, 3 March: Residents of Lakeview Lane report brown tap water for the third week in a row.
Complaint log, 9 March: Eleven households have now filed written complaints with the ward office.

Inspection report, 12 March: A water board engineer collected samples from four taps. The report states that iron levels were above the permitted limit in three of the four samples.

Official statement, 15 March: The ward office said the water is safe to drink and that the discolouration is temporary.

Repair record, 20 March: Contractors replaced a corroded 40-metre pipe section on Lakeview Lane. The record does not say whether water was re-tested afterwards.

Resident letter, 28 March: Residents say the water is clearer but still has a metallic smell. No new test results have been shared with them.
